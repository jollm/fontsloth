* Method

- =time= is wall-clock time for the measured call.
- =alloc= is an approximate byte count derived from the difference in
  =memory-use-counts= before and after the call.
- =retained= is the change in approximate used heap reported by
  =garbage-collect= after forcing a collection.
- =parse= is `fontsloth-otf-load-font-data`.
- =load+outline= is `fontsloth-load-font` with `:cache 'bypass`, which
  currently parses the font and eagerly outlines every cmap-reachable
  glyph.
- =raster-first= and =raster-second= rasterize the same code points twice.
  there is no raster cache, so both passes do equivalent work.

These numbers are for before/after comparison, not absolute benchmarks.
Allocation counts include Emacs internals and vary between runs.

* Observations

- SF Pro parse alone allocates ~6.3 GB and retains ~78 MB. Eager
  load+outline allocates ~14.6 GB and retains ~315 MB. Eager outlining
  is the dominant cost, as expected.
- The Plex subset shows the same shape at smaller scale: parse retains
  ~0.8 MB, while load+outline retains ~1.9 MB and allocates ~561 MB.
- Rasterization is cheap relative to loading/outlining at these sizes,
  but the per-glyph allocation is still significant for large fonts.
- The raw font bytes are retained by `fontsloth-otf-font` but not by the
  high-level `fontsloth-font`. The high-level retained figure is still
  large because of the fully outlined glyph vector.
